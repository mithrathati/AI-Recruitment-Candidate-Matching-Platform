Junior Data Analyst

We are hiring a Junior Data Analyst to join our analytics team and help drive data-informed business decisions. You will work with stakeholders across product, marketing, and operations to extract, analyze, and visualize data from multiple internal systems.

Responsibilities:
- Pull, clean, and analyze data from SQL databases and BI tools.
- Build dashboards and reports using Power BI / Tableau.
- Identify trends, KPIs, and actionable business insights.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to define analytics requirements.
- Automate repetitive reporting tasks using Python / Excel.

Required skills:
- 1+ year of hands-on experience in data analysis or a similar role.
- Strong SQL skills (joins, CTEs, window functions) with PostgreSQL or MySQL.
- Proficient with Excel (pivot tables, Power Query) and Power BI or Tableau.
- Python basics (Pandas, NumPy) for data manipulation.
- Good communication and presentation skills.
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Math, Economics, CS, or related field.

Nice-to-have:
- Experience with dbt, Airflow, Snowflake, or BigQuery.
- Exposure to A/B testing and statistical modeling.
- Knowledge of cloud data warehouses (AWS Redshift, GCP BigQuery).
